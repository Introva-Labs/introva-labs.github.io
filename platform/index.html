What the platform gives you

The advantages of a single system

One shared data model

Samples, results, instruments, people and records live in one model across all 17 products — so data flows between apps without brittle point-to-point integrations, and there is a single source of truth for every entity.

One design system

Every product shares the same components, navigation and interaction patterns. Your team learns the platform once, then moves between LIMS, QMS, ELN and the rest with zero relearning.

Compliance built in

Tamper-evident audit trails, witnessed reviews and 21 CFR Part 11 / EU Annex 11 electronic signatures are part of the platform — present in every module from day one, not bolted on per product.

Security & access control

Role-based access, SSO / SAML, and per-record authorization are enforced platform-wide. Multi-tenant isolation keeps each lab's and organization's data strictly separated.

A local AI copilot

LabAI answers from your own SOPs and knowledge base using on-prem retrieval — grounded in your documents, with your data never leaving your environment.

Global & deployment-flexible

Run fully on-premises or in the cloud. Multi-site, multi-currency and localization support scale the platform from a single bench to a global lab network.
