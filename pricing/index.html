Pricing questions

Why don't you list prices?
Lab software is modular and every lab is different — the right quote depends on which products you need, how many sites and users, and whether you run on-prem. We give you a tailored quote fast, with no surprise line items.
Can I start with just one product?
Yes. Every product works on its own, and you can add more at any time — data and users carry across the platform, so growing never means migrating.
How does the volume discount work?
Pricing is per product, and the effective per-product rate drops as you add more. The full 17-product suite earns the deepest discount.
Do you offer on-prem deployment?
Yes — run fully on-prem or in a private cloud, including the LabAI copilot with local retrieval so your data never leaves your environment.
Is the platform validated for GxP?
Audit trails and e-signatures aligned to 21 CFR Part 11 and EU Annex 11 are built into every module. A validation package (IQ/OQ/PQ) is available for regulated deployments.
What's included in a quote?
Implementation, onboarding, support and validation are scoped into every quote alongside your chosen products — so the number you see is the number you pay.
